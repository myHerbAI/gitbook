# Content status and provenance

myHerb preserves original work and its context. Reorganizing documentation must not erase authorship, change publication history or imply that later maintenance was part of the original work.

## Provenance rules

1. Keep the original author credit, dates, URLs and source links when they are known.
2. Label later editorial or technical maintenance separately.
3. Do not present a fork, example, software development kit or third-party repository as original myHerb intellectual property.
4. Do not call a draft or prototype a released product.
5. Distinguish verified facts from interpretation and future plans.

## Reliability labels

* **Verified:** supported by a current source or a preserved primary record.
* **Historical:** accurately preserved from an earlier period but not confirmed as current.
* **Needs verification:** plausible or previously stated, but not supported strongly enough for a current claim.
* **Planned:** a future direction, not an existing capability or offer.

## Aaron Levin's original work

Material originally created or published by Aaron Levin remains credited to him. New navigation, maintenance and future products are separate layers around that preserved work; they do not rewrite who created the original material.
