# Digital products

This section is the public documentation and preview layer for myHerb digital products. It does not process payment or grant access. Checkout, customer accounts and delivery belong on the main myHerb website.

## Product families

### Guides and playbooks

Curated, task-focused material derived from verified knowledge-base content. A guide is not for sale until the download, license, version, support route and refund terms are defined.

**Current status:** Planned.

### Templates and checklists

Reusable worksheets for sustainability planning, measurement and implementation.

**Current status:** Planned. Existing educational pages may mention templates, but that does not prove a packaged myHerb product is available.

### Courses and learning programs

Structured lessons with a defined audience, outcomes and completion journey.

**Current status:** Draft. The existing Sustainability: Getting Started program is not listed here as available for purchase until its content, access and end-to-end delivery have been verified.

### Tools and technical resources

Documented software, open-source resources and prototypes with clear maturity and license information.

**Current status:** Mixed. Check the [Tools & solutions directory](../knowledge-base/tools-and-solutions.md) for the status of each item.

## Availability promise

An item will be labelled **Available** only when the product page, price, checkout, payment, delivery, access, refund path, support route and legal terms have been tested together. Until then, the status shown here is authoritative.
